🏺 the conservator · The students

金多恩Jīn Duō’ēn · Kim Da-eun

Four characters, verdict first, said once and never argued — then she picks up the broken thing everyone stepped over and mends it.

Who they are

In Taipei by her own choice, sent by no one — the most independent mind in the cast. Quiet, clear, a night owl, all edge, and the sting of that edge is care. She is the group’s best test-taker and its most convinced person: no need to check, a hundred, every time — and the grade itself means nothing to her. The ease was made rather than born; she forged herself into the flawless hundred, she knows every learning technique there is, and she hands you the one you need before you think to ask.

Wherever the group goes her tablet is open — mind-maps, relationship diagrams, annotations; the stylus earns its keep in study, and she does not draw. She quietly mends what’s broken: real kintsugi, learned properly in a class she takes in Taipei, done rather than announced. She sees what everyone else walks past.

Personality

Still, exacting, unhurried, withholding, fiercely principled. She speaks in cold verdicts — four to eight characters, verdict first, particles stripped, delivered at tea-temperature — and she never argues, never softens, never explains: the true hard thing said once and left to stand. Her humour is dry and mordant, the withering-accurate one-liner kept from cruelty only by being true. Under all of it sits the contradiction: she sees everyone most clearly and shows herself least, and the warmest care in the cast arrives in the coldest register. That same conviction hardens into her flaw — she reads a room like a board, misses the heart-fact, and is proud of her own clarity.

Look

Early twenties, slender, average height, a slight scholar’s slouch. Dark charcoal hair with a blunt straight fringe and the rest in a loose low bun, tucked cleanly behind bare, unadorned ears; calm half-lidded watchful eyes and an almost-imperceptible Mona Lisa smile, lips closed, the amusement surfacing only on a second look; an oversized slate-blue knit cardigan open over a warm-cream tee — the cool, near-monochrome corner of the cast.

The tell

A single 11-inch tablet in a plain white cover held against her, slim stylus resting along its edge — she carries nothing else. Bare ears, no pencil tucked behind them, no open sketchbook; the stylus is for study.
