📷 the super-fan · The students

星野遙香Xīngyě Yáoxiāng · Hoshino Haruka

The Osaka super-fan who’s more into Taiwan than the locals are — camera up, a half-step ahead, and the reason five tired people go back out for one more stall.

Who they are

She came for the night markets, the live shows, the noise of the street, and she documents all of it on a film camera — the group’s documentarian, always the one behind the lens. She’s also the group’s energy: the half-step-ahead one who gets everybody out the door, first to try whatever the rest are dodging. Call her quiet and she’ll prove you wrong on the spot.

Personality

Loud, quick, delighted past all proportion — everything is 超~, and she’s occasionally self-aware about it. Over-excited about small finds — a new sticker, a stall she read about, a word she just learned — and she narrates her own life as it happens, out loud, like she’s filming it. She won’t let anyone sit the night out, dragging the group to one more stall before it ends. First to order the stinky tofu everyone else is dodging.

Look

Early twenties, bright and likeable. Dark chestnut hair in a short tousled bob with lived-in movement and face-framing strands, one side tucked behind her ear and pinned with a tiny gold star clip; warm freckled sun-kissed skin; a bright, natural smile, slightly open, the liveliness mostly in her eyes. An oversized relaxed oatmeal cardigan worn open over a cream tee, a slim solid indigo scarf, and one small muted-persimmon leather tag as a warm signature.

The tell

A small retro silver-and-mint film camera on a strap across her chest, one round smiley sticker on its body; the tiny gold star clip in her hair is the secondary mark. The camera-at-chest silhouette reads her from across a frame before you see her face.
